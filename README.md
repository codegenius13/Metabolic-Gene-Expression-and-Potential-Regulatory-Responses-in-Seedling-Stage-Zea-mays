# Comparative Transcriptomic Analysis of Metabolic Gene Expression and Potential Regulatory Responses in Seedling-Stage Maize (*Zea mays*) under Drought, Salinity, Heat, and Cold Stress

## Project Overview

This repository contains the computational workflow, processed datasets, analysis scripts, and supplementary results associated with a comparative transcriptomic reanalysis of seedling-stage maize under drought, salinity, heat, and cold stress.

The study examines stress-responsive gene expression, enriched biological processes, metabolic enzyme candidates, and transcription factor responses across the four stress conditions. It further explores the overlap between metabolic candidates, transcriptional regulators, and enriched biological processes to provide an integrated view of potential molecular responses.

## Data Source

The analysis uses publicly available maize RNA-seq data from:

- **Original study:** Li et al. (2017)
- **Project accession:** PRJNA335771
- **SRA study:** SRP080208
- **Organism:** *Zea mays*
- **Reference:** [Li et al. (2017)](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2017.00290)

Raw sequencing data are available through the NCBI Sequence Read Archive. They are not duplicated in this repository.
